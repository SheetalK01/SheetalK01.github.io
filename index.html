about me

A little about me

I'm from Odisha. I did my BCA from Presidency College, Berhampur and my MCA from NIST Berhampur. After that I joined KodNest for their Full Stack Java program, which is where I really got into Java, Spring Boot and building web apps.

My first job was as a QA Engineer at Zenalyst.AI, a fintech startup in Bengaluru. I started the Playwright automation there and kept growing it until it covered most of the product. Because it was a small team I also picked up some frontend and backend tasks, and I liked that a lot.

In my free time I built two projects around AI and testing, TestPilot AI and EvalPilot, mostly to learn how AI can help in testing and how AI features themselves should be tested.

EvalPilot

Testing tool for AI chatbots

You add test cases for your chatbot, run them and watch the results come in live, compare two runs to find what broke, and run attack prompts (like prompt injection) to check if the bot is safe. It also has a small npm SDK to log real chatbot calls and a CLI that can fail a CI build if the pass rate drops.

Tests: 700+ with Jest, Vitest, Pact, Playwright and k6
